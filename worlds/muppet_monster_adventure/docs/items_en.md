## Item

The Muppet Monster Adventure randomizer has the follow items:

* **Abilities** - These are **Progression** and are what lets you actually do things
  - *Glide* - The ability to glide from a high location to a lower location,
               preferably towards something you couldn't just walk to
  - *Climb* - The ability to climb up cylindical pillars that are perfect cat scratch posts
  - *Push*  - The ability to push conveniently placed, perfectly shaped cubes, along pre-destined paths
  - *Swim*  - The ability to swim under the water, this is generally considered inconvenient
  - *Smash* - The ability to open things with a well timed chop, you probably could have knocked
  - *Glove* - The ability to shoot people from close range, in muppet law, this is considered rude
  - *Spin*  - The ability to turn around quickly without getting dizzy, pretty impressive

* **Levels** - These are **Progression**, levels marked with a * are able to be one of the starting levels
  - *Normal levels*
    * *Peacock Purgatory
    * *Hallways of Doom
    * *Poker Faces
    * Grave Matters
    * Molten Mayhem
    * Shivering Timber Shoals
    * Hike of the Haunted
    * The River Vile
    * Escape Claws
    * Croak-Lahoma
    * Arabian Frights
    * Feeling Flushed
    * Hut, Hut, Hike!
    * Temple of Pork
    * The Bluest Bayou
    * The Monstery Monastery
    * Ice to Meetcha!
    * For Peton's Sake
  - *Boss levels*
    * Noseferatu Bites Back!
    * Bee-ware the WereBear!
    * Cower Before... Ker-Monster!
    * The Muck Monster Slimeth
    * Doin' the Bride Slide
    * The Mystery of... The Master!

* **Filler** - These items are **Filler** items, and are beneficial to your adventure, but are usually reset on death
  - *Yip Yip Yip* - Heal one health, up to your maximum health
  - *Delicious Cookie! Om nom nom* - The cookie is so tasty your maximum health is increased by 1
  - *Kissy Kissy* - Miss Piggy gives you a kiss, granting you an extra life
  - *Ethel's Rifle* - Ethel the Chicken gives you 15 seconds of the Chicken Glove power
  - *Stephanie's Shotgun* - Stephanie the Chicken has more ammo, so you get 30 seconds of the Chicken Glove power [Normal default Chicken length]
  - *Camilla's Cannon* - Camilla the Chicken enjoys a bit of mayhem, granting you 1 minute of the Chicken Glove power
  - *Hoobygoop Covered Hoobybun* - These Hoobybuns are so tasty, you'll fully heal!

* **Trap** - These items are **Trap** items, and usually hinder your adventure, but are usually reset on death
  - *Heckled by Statler and Waldorf* - Statler and Waldorf aren't always so nice, and it hurts you a little (minimum of 0)
  - *Bunsen's Failed Experiment* - Bunsen's experiment fails causing your maximum health to decrease by 1 (minimum of 0)
  - *Maniacal Laugh* - Tex Richman wants to stop you succeeding, and takes away one of your lives (minimum of 0)
  - *Cooked Chook* - Whatever chicken power you had, it now only lasts 1 second
  - *Shaken Tailfeather* - Your chicken power is very limited, only 2 seconds long
  - *Fowl Words* - Your chicken power has some bad things to say, and 5 seconds to say about
  - *One Hit K/O* - This sets your current HP to 0, one hit away from dying
