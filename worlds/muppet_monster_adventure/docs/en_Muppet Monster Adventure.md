## What does randomization do to this game?

1. The first 3 levels (Insert level names here) have Amulets, in addition all 
   BONUS letters and Muppet Tokens are randomised, and can be found in the other 
   levels.

2. All locations can contain items from other worlds, and any items from 
   your world can appear in other players' worlds.

3. By default, you will start with the Claw and Spin attacks, in addition to a 
   single starting level

4. Additional locations include:
   * Defeating each of the 6 bosses
   * 50% and 100% energy collected in each level

5. Locations do not indicate if they are randomised or not visually
   (they will appear as their default object).
   Refer to the text client for any items received or sent.

## What's the goal?

Your goal is to defeat a number of bosses, by default this will be 5 of them
